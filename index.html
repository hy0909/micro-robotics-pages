03 — Technology

Four capabilities.
One precise loop.

From first signal to controlled action, our research connects perception, sensing, control and edge intelligence.

01

AI Detection & Tracking

Perception models identify small, fast-moving targets and maintain a continuous trajectory in complex scenes.

02

Sensor & Optics

Purpose-built sensing and optical configurations capture the signal needed for reliable micro-target observation.

03

Precision Control

Low-latency control translates target coordinates into accurate, repeatable physical response.

04

Edge AI & Safety

On-device inference, bounded operation and layered safeguards keep the system fast, local and controlled.
